Bio & Research Vision

I am a research engineer specializing in autonomous robotics, optimal control theory, and machine learning. My work focuses on closing the perception-action loop on real-world embedded hardware—combining first-principles dynamical modeling with data-driven reinforcement learning policies.

🤖
Core Research Focus
  • Non-linear Model Predictive Control (NMPC) & trajectory optimization
  • Real-time embedded vision and state estimation for multi-rotor aerial vehicles
  • Safe reinforcement learning with control barrier functions (CBFs)
  • Edge compute deployment (WebAssembly, C++20, ROS 2, Linux RT-Preempt)

Experience & Career Trajectory

2024 — Present
Senior Autonomous Systems Engineer
Cybernetics Robotics Institute • Munich, Germany
Leading research into agile multi-agent trajectory tracking and neural MPC for aerial vehicles. Architected high-throughput hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) simulation testbeds running under 200 Hz control loops.
2022 — 2024
Robotics Software Engineer
Autonomous Rover Dynamics • Zurich, Switzerland
Developed sensor fusion algorithms (Extended Kalman Filter / Factor Graphs) integrating LiDAR, IMU, and RTK-GNSS on wheeled mobile rovers. Implemented safety-critical CAN bus communication and low-level brushless motor drive firmware.
2020 — 2022
Machine Learning Research Fellow
Autonomous Systems Laboratory • Berlin, Germany
Researched sample-efficient reinforcement learning algorithms for robotic arm manipulation and tactile slip detection. Published 2 peer-reviewed conference articles at ICRA and IROS.
