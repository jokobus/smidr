## Bio & Research Vision

I am a research engineer specializing in **autonomous robotics, optimal control theory, and machine learning**. My work focuses on closing the perception-action loop on real-world embedded hardware—combining first-principles dynamical modeling with data-driven reinforcement learning policies.

{{< alert type="robotics" title="Core Research Focus" >}}
- Non-linear Model Predictive Control (NMPC) & trajectory optimization
- Real-time embedded vision and state estimation for multi-rotor aerial vehicles
- Safe reinforcement learning with control barrier functions (CBFs)
- Edge compute deployment (WebAssembly, C++20, ROS 2, Linux RT-Preempt)
{{< /alert >}}
