Real-Time Neural MPC for Agile Quadrotor Flight in Cluttered Environments

J. Smidr, A. Turing, K. Robotics
📄 PDF 🔗 DOI 💻 Code
📑 Abstract & BibTeX

Abstract: We present an ultra-fast neural model predictive control (MPC) policy trained through reinforcement learning with closed-loop stability verification. Operating on an embedded compute unit, the proposed policy tracks acrobatic trajectories at 45 m/s with sub-centimeter drift.
